# Tests

## Test 1 - Mobile Watch fits the phone and opens power features in focused sections [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: direct /watch shell delivery, phone-width layout, compact action buttons, Ask Sermon sheet, Watch Together sheet and Growth sheet
Description: Verifies the Watch page no longer clips titles or actions and advanced features stay out of the main page until the member taps them.

Steps:
1. Open NFCPS Watch on mobile, open a sermon, and review the title, creator row and Like, Share, Save, Ask sermon, Watch together and Grow actions
2. Tap Ask sermon, close its focused section, then tap Grow and close that section
3. Tap Watch together and review the room setup section

Expected: The sermon page fits inside the phone width without clipped controls or horizontal page overflow, the main page stays concise, and Ask sermon, Grow and Watch together each open in their own focused section.

## Test 2 - A shared Watch Together link lets a second member actually join the room
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: /api/watch/rooms creation and join routes, shareable room link, guest join gate, realtime WebSocket entity subscription, participant presence and synchronized playback
Description: Verifies Watch Together behaves like a real shareable room instead of exposing room state before the guest has joined.

Steps:
1. Actor A opens Watch Together, enters "Ada", creates a room and taps Share; note the six-character room code
2. Actor B opens the shared room link on another client, enters "Chidi" on the Join room screen and joins
3. Actor A plays and pauses together, sends a reaction and message; Actor B replies

Expected: Actor B sees a clean Join room screen before entering, both members then appear in the same room, playback state synchronizes, and shared reactions and messages update without manual refresh.

## Test 3 - Ask Sermon stays grounded and returns to the exact sermon moment
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
