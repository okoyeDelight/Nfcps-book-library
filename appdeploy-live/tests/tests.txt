1. Open You, tap Sign in, switch to Create account, and enter a valid name with an invalid email and a password shorter than 8 characters

Description: Verifies Watch prefers reusable intelligence but does not get stuck on sermons without captions or a trusted audio source; the viewer can explicitly enable speaker-word recognition once and NFCPS then reuses that preference for future unsupported sermons.

Steps:
1. Open Divine Prosperity (Part 1) by Lawrence Oyor. Confirm the glass Scripture Lens prepares from its trusted source without microphone permission, continues preparing later playback windows, and surfaces canonical Scripture as mapped sections become ready.
2. Open a sermon that has neither a reusable transcript nor a registered trusted source. Confirm the Lens offers Enable Spoken Lens instead of remaining permanently stuck; enable it, grant microphone permission, and confirm recognized speaker words can produce live Scripture context. After enough spoken context is learned, confirm the Lens changes to a prepared state and the learned transcript is reusable.
3. Open another unsupported sermon and confirm a previously successful Spoken Lens opt-in can resume automatically; pause it from the Lens to revoke that preference. Open deeper study/Ask This Sermon and Live Church Lens, then confirm Shorts still expose sound control plus replenishment.

Expected: Watch follows the priority cached/learned transcript → reusable captions/trusted source → explicit speaker-word recognition fallback, raw audio is not stored or uploaded by the web layer, successful learned text becomes reusable sermon intelligence, canonical KJV text is retrieved separately from AI, and the viewer remains in control of microphone listening.
