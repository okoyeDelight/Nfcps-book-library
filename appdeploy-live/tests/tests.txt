# Tests

## Test 1 - Premium in-app account screen stays inside NFCPS One [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: in-app account board, Sign in/Create account switch, mobile-safe layout, password visibility control, anonymous continuation, no popup-dependent main login
Description: Verifies the redesigned account experience behaves like a native app screen and keeps every primary control usable on a phone.

Steps:
1. Open You and tap Sign in
2. Confirm the account screen shows NFCPS One identity, a large Sign in title, Sign in/Create account control, grouped email/password fields, Continue button and Continue without an account
3. Switch to Create account, toggle password visibility, then switch back to Sign in and close the screen

Expected: The account experience remains entirely inside NFCPS One, all controls fit within the mobile viewport and are tappable, mode switching is stable, and closing or continuing anonymously returns to the app without losing local state.

## Test 2 - Account validation remains clear and non-destructive
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: client validation guardrail, inline account error presentation, local-first safety
Description: Verifies the visual redesign did not weaken account validation or clear device data on an invalid attempt.

Steps:
1. Open Create account and enter a valid name with an invalid email address and a password shorter than 8 characters
2. Submit the form and inspect the account screen
3. Correct the email but leave the short password, submit again, then close the account screen

Expected: Clear inline validation remains on the account screen, no external browser opens, no account is created from invalid data, and the rest of NFCPS One remains usable.

## Test 3 - Swipe pages like a physical book and keep reader preferences
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: swipe-driven page navigation, 3D page-flip presentation, page-turn sound preference, reader safe-area exit control, system UI font stack, reader typography isolation
Description: Verifies page mode behaves like a book rather than relying on next/previous buttons, while existing reader preferences remain intact.

Steps:
1. Open The Pursuit of God in Pages mode, confirm the back button is fully inside the left edge, then swipe left across the reading page and swipe right
2. Let the reading chrome fade and confirm the back button remains reachable, then reveal controls, open Themes & Settings and turn Page turn sound off and back on
3. Switch the reading font between Serif and Sans, close the book with the safe back control and reopen it

Expected: Horizontal swipes turn pages with the page-flip presentation, no page-navigation arrow buttons are required, the reader exit control never clips off-screen and remains reachable when reading chrome fades, the Page turn sound preference persists, and the selected book font still changes independently of the app UI font.

## Test 4 - Existing account create/sign-in and cloud merge still work after UI polish
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: POST /api/nfcps-account/create, POST /api/nfcps-account/sign-in, POST /api/member-sync/get, POST /api/member-sync/save, session persistence, local/cloud merge
Description: Guards the existing authentication and sync behavior while only its presentation is redesigned.

Steps:
1. While signed out, open a book and create local reading progress, then open the account screen and create an NFCPS account with valid details
2. Confirm the account becomes signed in and sync completes, then sign out
3. Sign back in to the same account and reopen the book

Expected: Account creation and sign-in still complete successfully, the session persists as before, and pre-login reading progress remains available after cloud sync and sign-in.

## Test 5 - Deep Read catalogue grows continuously and immersive Watch exits stay safe
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: GET /api/ebooks/catalog pagination, infinite catalogue discovery, progressive rendering, Watch selected-video back control, Shorts back control, full-screen safe-area navigation
Description: Verifies the Read shelf can keep discovering more public-domain titles without a small hard cap and that other immersive screens keep a reachable exit.

Steps:
1. Open Read with the All filter and scroll repeatedly to the bottom sentinel until the discovered-title count increases beyond the initially loaded shelf
2. Continue scrolling and confirm more book cards are appended automatically without a More button, then open and close a book detail screen
3. Open Watch, enter a sermon, return with the top-left back control, open Shorts and return with its top-left back control

Expected: Read progressively appends paged catalogue results as the member approaches the bottom, existing books remain stable while more are added, and book detail, Watch video and Shorts back controls stay fully inside the mobile safe area and return to the previous NFCPS screen.
