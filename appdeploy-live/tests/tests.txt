# Tests

## Test 1 - Premium account flow stays in-app and validates safely [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: in-app account board, Sign in/Create account switch, client validation guardrail, password visibility control, anonymous continuation, mobile-safe layout
Description: Verifies the account experience remains native-feeling and invalid credentials do not create an account or damage local state.

Steps:
1. Open You, tap Sign in, switch to Create account, and enter a valid name with an invalid email and a password shorter than 8 characters
2. Submit, confirm inline validation appears, then correct the email while keeping the short password and submit again
3. Toggle password visibility, switch back to Sign in, then close the account screen

Expected: Validation remains inside NFCPS One, no invalid account is created, all controls stay reachable on mobile, and closing the screen returns to the app without clearing local state.

## Test 5 - Watch intelligence connects sermons to Scripture without replacing Scripture
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: GET /api/watch/feed, GET /api/watch/shorts/feed, POST /api/watch/sermon/:id/speech-lens, GET /api/watch/speech-intelligence/heartbeat, GET /api/watch/sermon/:id/intelligence, POST /api/watch/sermon/:id/ask, POST /api/watch/live-lens, automatic speech-first sermon learning, auto-start glass Scripture Lens, Android native speech bridge, iframe playback-time fallback, dismiss/reopen Lens pill, optional caption shortcut, autonomous Watch discovery, canonical KJV retrieval, Sermon Map, claim comparison, Scripture Trail, knowledge graph, Live Church Lens, growth-balanced recommendation, sound-on endless Shorts, safe fallback behavior
Description: Verifies Watch can autonomously supply Christian media and add a Scripture-aware study layer while keeping AI separate from canonical Bible text.

Steps:
1. Open Watch and start a sermon. Confirm the glass Scripture Lens appears automatically over the player without waiting for a reported YouTube timestamp or tapping a Scripture Lens/Sermon Map button. On native Android, confirm the native speech bridge is preferred; otherwise use browser speech recognition. If microphone permission is requested, allow it once, then let the message play through multiple recognized speech sections and confirm related canonical Scripture appears automatically.
2. Collapse the glass Lens and confirm a small Lens live pill remains; reopen it, then open the deeper study view, jump from the Sermon Map to a timestamp, inspect a claim with Read With/Compare passages, save a KJV passage, and ask which Scriptures support the sermon point.
3. Confirm the sermon intelligence UI never presents caption availability as the requirement for Scripture Lens. Return to Watch, open Live Scripture Lens, examine a short teaching statement alongside Scripture, then open Shorts and confirm visible sound control plus replenishment.

Expected: Scripture Lens behaves as an ambient part of sermon playback rather than a manually started tool, appears even when the YouTube iframe cannot report current time, prefers the Android native speech bridge when available, falls back to browser recognition elsewhere, requests microphone permission only when required, learns the speaker’s actual words without depending on YouTube captions, maintains usable fallback timestamps, remains transparently visible as a live pill when collapsed, retrieves canonical KJV text separately from AI, grows Sermon Map/Claims/Trail in the background, preserves safe theological framing, syncs saved Scripture and discipleship memory, and leaves trusted Watch/Shorts usable if AI or external services fail.
