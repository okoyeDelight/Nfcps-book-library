# Tests

## Test 1 - Concept Home to Read to Book Detail to Reader [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: Android-first NFCPS One Home composition, five-tab dock, Read shelves, locally-owned e-book catalogue, /api/ebooks/catalog enrichment, Book Detail, /api/reader/book, reader progress
Description: Verifies the primary concept flow matches the approved Living Sanctuary direction while preserving real reading behavior.

Steps:
1. Open NFCPS One and confirm the Home screen has the compact scenic greeting, one dominant book focus, Continue action, For You Today and a correctly aligned five-tab dock without a separate floating top chrome block
2. Tap Read and confirm the page switches to the bright editorial layout with Continue Reading, search, category chips and multiple cover-led shelves
3. Tap The Pursuit of God, confirm the Book Detail screen appears, then tap Read and advance one page

Expected: Home fits naturally without a large dead middle, Read presents many books with designed covers, Book Detail precedes reading, and the real reader opens and updates progress.

Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: 23-book local NFCPS catalogue, /api/ebooks/catalog failure behavior, search and category browsing, reader fallback
Description: Verifies the reading experience never collapses to a tiny or blank shelf when the external catalogue is unavailable.

```json
[

    "method": "GET",
    "path": "/api/ebooks/catalog",

  }
]
```

Steps:
1. Open Read while catalogue requests fail
2. Browse For You, Christian Classics, Prayer, Bible & Study and All Books
3. Search for Pursuit and open the matching title

Expected: The locally-owned collection remains visibly populated across shelves and search, and a known title still opens Book Detail and the reader.
