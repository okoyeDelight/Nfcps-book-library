# Tests

## Test 1 - Premium account flow stays in-app and validates safely [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: in-app account board, Sign in/Create account switch, client validation guardrail, password visibility control, anonymous continuation, mobile-safe layout
Description: Verifies the account experience remains native-feeling and invalid credentials do not create an account or damage local state.

Steps:
1. Open You, tap Sign in, switch to Create account, and enter a valid name with an invalid email and a password shorter than 8 characters
2. Submit, confirm inline validation appears, then correct the email while keeping the short password and submit again
3. Toggle password visibility, switch back to Sign in, then close the account screen

Expected: Validation remains inside NFCPS One, no invalid account is created, all controls stay reachable on mobile, and closing the screen returns to the app without clearing local state.

## Test 5 - Watch intelligence connects sermons to Scripture without replacing Scripture
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: GET /api/watch/feed, GET /api/watch/shorts/feed, GET /api/watch/sermon/:id/intelligence, POST /api/watch/sermon/:id/ask, POST /api/watch/live-lens, autonomous Watch discovery, Scripture Lens timed cards, canonical KJV retrieval, Sermon Map, claim comparison, Scripture Trail, knowledge graph, Live Church Lens, growth-balanced recommendation, sound-on endless Shorts, safe fallback behavior
Description: Verifies Watch can autonomously supply Christian media and add a Scripture-aware study layer while keeping AI separate from canonical Bible text.

Steps:
1. Open Watch, open a captioned sermon, and confirm Scripture Lens can prepare the message package, show a timed Scripture card during playback, and open Lens, Map, Claims, Trail, Connections, and Ask views
2. Jump from the Sermon Map to a timestamp, inspect a claim with Read With/Compare passages, save a KJV passage, then ask which Scriptures support the sermon point and confirm transcript timestamps plus canonical Scripture cards are returned
3. Return to Watch, open Live Scripture Lens, type a short teaching statement into its fallback box and examine it alongside Scripture; then open Shorts, confirm visible sound control and swipe far enough to trigger replenishment

Expected: Verse text displayed by Scripture Lens comes from the Bible retrieval layer rather than model-generated quotations, the agent describes support/context/compare relationships without declaring a preacher spiritually approved or condemned, sermon intelligence is cached for reuse, saved passages feed discipleship memory, recommendations remain diverse and growth-balanced, Live Church Lens has a usable text fallback when microphone transcription is unsupported, and trusted Watch/Shorts content remains available if AI or external services fail.
