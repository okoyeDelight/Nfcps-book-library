# Tests

## Test 1 - Free e-books always show and open inside NFCPS [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: native/live shell delivery, prominent Watch and Free E-books shortcuts, /api/ebooks/catalog backend catalog proxy, visible free e-book shelf, Read action, /api/reader/book Gutenberg copy retrieval, reader pagination and saved progress
Description: Verifies an installed/native-style session receives the current shell and can immediately reach the new e-book and Watch experiences.

Steps:
1. Open the app with ?source=native and confirm the upper shortcut area visibly includes FREE E-BOOKS · LIVE and NFCPS WATCH · LIVE
2. Open Free E-books, confirm multiple Project Gutenberg books are visible, then tap Read now and advance to another page
3. Close the reader and reopen the same title from Continue Reading

Expected: The current shell exposes Watch and e-books immediately, the shelf is not blank, the selected book opens inside NFCPS, page navigation works, and reopening returns to approximately the saved reading position.

## Test 2 - E-book catalog failure falls back to the NFCPS starter shelf
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: e-book catalog failure guardrail, local starter shelf fallback, visible Read and Download actions
Description: Verifies an upstream catalog outage cannot leave the e-book section empty.

QA Faults:
```json
[
  {
    "method": "GET",
    "path": "/api/ebooks/catalog",
    "status": 500,
    "body_json": { "error": "simulated_catalog_outage" }
  }
]
```

Steps:
1. Open the Free E-books section while the catalog request fails
2. Review the visible starter shelf and outage notice
3. Confirm a starter title still exposes Read now and Download

Expected: A visible NFCPS starter shelf replaces the failed catalog response, an explanatory notice is shown, and the section never becomes a blank area.

## Test 3 - Advanced reader controls remain available
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: reader appearance settings, Book Map search, bookmarks, multi-colour highlights, notes, immersive mode and per-book persistence
Description: Verifies the catalog fix does not regress the novel-style reader experience.

Steps:
1. Open a free e-book and change typography, line spacing, page margins, brightness, text colour and page theme
2. Search inside the book, save a bookmark, add two highlight colours and one note
3. Activate immersive reading, exit it, close the book and reopen it

Expected: Search and annotations work, immersive mode has a clear exit, and the book reopens with the saved reading appearance and study state.

## Test 4 - Watch feed keeps extending as the user scrolls
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: /api/watch/feed trusted content, creator-interleaved long-form feed, automatic infinite-scroll batching, Watch Later and Continue Watching
Description: Verifies NFCPS Watch behaves like a continuing feed instead of stopping after the first fixed group of videos.

Steps:
1. Open NFCPS Watch and scroll through the For You feed until the initial visible batch is nearly exhausted
2. Continue scrolling past the loading-more sentinel into the next batch
3. Save and open one video, then close it and review Watch Later and Continue Watching

Expected: More video cards are appended automatically without an end-of-feed state, creators remain mixed when alternatives are available, and saved/history state remains usable.

## Test 5 - Shorts continue beyond the first batch with creator-aware rotation
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: daily themed Shorts, full-height vertical snap scrolling, automatic Shorts extension, creator-aware reshuffling and trusted YouTube playback
Description: Verifies the Shorts experience continues loading as the user swipes rather than ending after a fixed list.

Steps:
1. Open Shorts and note today's message/theme
2. Swipe through enough clips to pass the initial Shorts batch
3. Keep swiping into the automatically appended clips and inspect the creator sequence

Expected: Shorts continue beyond the initial batch with no terminal end screen, the daily theme remains consistent, vertical snapping remains full-height, and different trusted creators are rotated whenever alternatives exist.
