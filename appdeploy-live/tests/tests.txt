# Tests

## Test 1 - Premium account flow stays in-app and validates safely [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: in-app account board, Sign in/Create account switch, client validation guardrail, password visibility control, anonymous continuation, mobile-safe layout
Description: Verifies the account experience remains native-feeling and invalid credentials do not create an account or damage local state.

Steps:
1. Open You, tap Sign in, switch to Create account, and enter a valid name with an invalid email and a password shorter than 8 characters
2. Submit, confirm inline validation appears, then correct the email while keeping the short password and submit again
3. Toggle password visibility, switch back to Sign in, then close the account screen

Expected: Validation remains inside NFCPS One, no invalid account is created, all controls stay reachable on mobile, and closing the screen returns to the app without clearing local state.

## Test 5 - Watch stays Christian, varied, and increasingly personal
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: GET /api/watch/feed, trusted plus guarded discovered catalogue merge, Christian category expansion, local recommendation ranking, creator diversity, likes/saves/subscriptions/history, Shorts creator interleaving, safe fallback behavior
Description: Verifies Watch can accept safely discovered Christian content without breaking the existing catalogue, while For You responds to member signals and avoids repetitive creator runs.

Steps:
1. Open Watch and confirm the hero, For You rail, creator names, Christian categories, and Shorts all load even if external discovery is temporarily unavailable
2. Open a message, Like it, Save it, Follow its creator, return to Watch, then open another message from a related category and return again

Expected: Existing trusted content remains available as a fallback, For You increasingly favors followed creators and relevant categories without filling the rail with one creator when alternatives exist, Shorts rotate across creators instead of running long same-creator blocks, and no candidate needs autonomous code deployment to enter the catalogue.
