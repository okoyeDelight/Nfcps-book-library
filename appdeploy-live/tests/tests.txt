# Tests

## Test 1 - Daily Watch opens a message and records meaningful growth [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: current installed shell delivery, /api/watch/feed, NFCPS Daily Watch, dedicated watch page, Sermon Companion, note persistence, response tracking and growth rhythm
Description: Verifies the new difference layer turns a normal video into a guided spiritual next step without breaking the YouTube-style player.

Steps:
1. Open NFCPS Watch and start the visible NFCPS Daily Watch card
2. On the watch page review Key Scripture, Reflect and Take Action, write a note, choose “I’m responding”, then complete Daily Watch
3. Return to You and review the growth scoreboard, then refresh the page and reopen the same video

Expected: The Daily Watch opens normally, the Sermon Companion is visible below the player, the note and response persist after refresh, today is marked complete and the growth rhythm is reflected in You.

## Test 2 - Growth goals reshape the Home feed while keeping creator diversity
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: growth-goal selection, local goal persistence, recommendation weighting, creator-aware diversity and Home feed
Description: Verifies a user can tell NFCPS what they want to grow in and the feed adapts without becoming a single-creator tunnel.

Steps:
1. In Home choose Prayer and Know the Word under Shape my growth feed
2. Browse the next visible Home feed cards and note the mix of relevant categories and creators
3. Refresh the page and return to Home

Expected: The selected growth goals remain active after refresh, relevant categories are prioritised in the feed, and multiple trusted creators continue to appear.

## Test 3 - Growth Journeys progress one day at a time and persist
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: guided Growth Journeys, journey video selection, journey-day completion, persistent progress and You growth summary
Description: Verifies NFCPS Watch can guide a user through a defined discipleship path instead of only offering an endless feed.

Steps:
1. Start Build a Prayer Life from the Growth Journeys section
2. Watch the selected journey message and tap Complete journey day in the Sermon Companion
3. Return to You, confirm the journey shows 1/7 days, refresh, and open the journey again

Expected: The first journey day completes once, 1/7 progress remains after refresh, the growth scoreboard updates and Continue journey selects the next journey step.

## Test 5 - Watch failure fallback still preserves the growth experience and Reading bridge
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: /api/watch/feed failure fallback, trusted seed content, Daily Watch fallback, Sermon Companion and NFCPS reader bridge
Description: Verifies the new growth layer remains usable even when fresh Watch data temporarily fails.

QA Faults:
```json
[
  {
    "method": "GET",
    "path": "/api/watch/feed",
    "status": 500,
    "body_json": { "error": "simulated_watch_outage" }
  }
]
```

Steps:
1. Open NFCPS Watch while the Watch feed request fails and confirm trusted fallback videos plus Daily Watch still appear
2. Open a fallback teaching and review its Sermon Companion
3. Tap Go deeper in Reading and open a visible free e-book

Expected: Watch does not become blank during the simulated outage, the growth companion remains usable on fallback content, and the user can still move into the NFCPS e-book reader.
