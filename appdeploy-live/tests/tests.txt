# Tests

## Test 1 - Main account opens and authenticates entirely inside NFCPS One [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: in-app NFCPS One account board, account creation, account sign-in, session persistence, no popup-dependent main login
Description: Verifies the main account flow is an app-owned board rather than the external popup that previously escaped to Chrome.

Steps:
1. Open You and tap Sign in, then confirm the full-screen NFCPS One account board appears inside the current app with Sign in and Create account tabs
2. Choose Create account and create a fresh QA account using a valid test name, email and an 8+ character test password
3. Sign out, tap Sign in again, and sign back into that same QA account

Expected: Account creation and sign-in complete in the NFCPS One interface, the account becomes signed in after both flows, and the main account experience does not depend on a separate Chrome or AppDeploy popup window.

## Test 2 - Existing phone activity merges into the NFCPS account and restores
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: member sync read/write, local/cloud merge, reader progress, bookmarks, notes, Watch saves, account session
Description: Verifies changing the login architecture does not lose the local-first data model.

Steps:
1. While signed out, open The Pursuit of God, advance several pages, add a bookmark and save a Watch message
2. Sign in using the in-app NFCPS One account board and wait for sync to complete
3. Sign out, sign back in to the same account and reopen Read and Watch Later

Expected: The pre-login reading position/bookmark and saved Watch state remain available after sign-in and after the account is reopened.

## Test 3 - Invalid credentials stay inside the board and preserve device data
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: account validation guardrail, rejected sign-in, non-destructive local-first failure state
Description: Verifies a failed sign-in is handled as an in-app form error instead of navigating away or clearing local data.

Steps:
1. Open the NFCPS One sign-in board and enter a known QA email with an incorrect password
2. Submit the form and inspect the account board
3. Close the board and reopen the most recently read book

Expected: A visible email/password error remains on the NFCPS One board, no external login page is required, and existing reading progress on the device is unchanged.

## Test 4 - Read, Watch and physical Library remain intact after account replacement
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: five-tab shell, Read reader, Watch navigation, physical Library, anonymous mode after account refactor
Description: Guards the rest of NFCPS One while the account transport changes.

Steps:
1. Use NFCPS One without signing in and open a real e-book in Read
2. Leave Read for Watch, then return and open the physical Library
3. Return to You and open the new account board without submitting it

Expected: Read, Watch and Library continue to work anonymously, and opening or closing the account board does not disturb those workflows.
