# Reader V3 — 8 Sep 2026

Live AppDeploy build: https://nfcps-book-library-c2ma7y.v2.appdeploy.ai/

This stage upgrades the NFCPS free e-book experience into a novel-style reader:

- Project Gutenberg cards expose clear Read and Download actions.
- Page-based reading replaces the old endless-scroll reading surface.
- Previous / Next navigation, page count and reading percentage.
- Per-book reading controls: font size, line spacing, serif/sans typeface, paper/sepia/light/dark page themes.
- Bookmarks, selected-text highlights, passage notes and a private Notes & Highlights panel.
- Continue Reading restores reading progress and appearance preferences.
- Successful public-domain opens continue to cache locally for offline continuation where supported.
- NFCPS Moments continues receiving excerpt context from the reader's current page.
- Modern copyrighted recommendations remain information-only; public-domain classics are labelled Read / Download.
- The installable app manifest references the official NFCPS logo and now marks it for normal and maskable icon use.

Reader text is served through the NFCPS `/api/reader/book/:id` Gutenberg proxy to reduce browser CORS failures.
